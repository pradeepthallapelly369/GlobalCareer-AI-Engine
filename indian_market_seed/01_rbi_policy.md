# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Draft Statement

## Internal Memo on Inflation Projections
Date: April 5, 2026

**Context:** The MPC has reviewed the latest Consumer Price Index (CPI) numbers, which indicate a spike in retail inflation to 6.2%, breaching the upper tolerance band of 6%. This is primarily driven by an unforeseen supply shock in the agricultural sector, causing widespread food inflation, particularly in cereals and pulses. 

**Proposed Action:** To anchor inflation expectations and defend the INR against the strengthening USD, the committee is debating an unexpected 50 basis points (bps) hike to the Repo Rate, bringing it to 7.00%. 

**Potential Market Impact:** 
- A rate hike of this magnitude is not currently priced into the bond markets.
- Yields on the 10-year benchmark government bond are expected to spike.
- Banking sector margins might initially expand, but credit growth could slow down significantly.
- Auto and Real Estate sectors, heavily reliant on consumer loans, will likely face immediate headwinds.
