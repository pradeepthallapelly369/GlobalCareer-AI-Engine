# FII and DII Market Flow Data

## Weekly Institutional Sentiment Report
Date: April 5, 2026

**Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs):**
- FIIs have been net sellers in the Indian equity markets for the past 5 consecutive trading sessions.
- Outflow reason: The US Federal Reserve has signaled that rates will remain "higher for longer," prompting capital reallocation away from emerging markets back to US Treasury bonds.
- FIIs are heavily unwinding their long positions in Indian Financials and IT sectors.

**Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs):**
- DIIs, largely fueled by robust Retail SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) inflows, continue to be aggregate net buyers.
- Retail participation remains at an all-time high, creating a floor against the massive FII sell-off.
- However, mutual fund managers are increasing cash allocations, opting for a defensive stance due to mounting global volatility and stretched mid-cap valuations.

**Retail Investor Sentiment:** 
- Highly optimistic, viewing every dip as a buying opportunity, primarily heavily invested in Small and Mid-cap indices.
