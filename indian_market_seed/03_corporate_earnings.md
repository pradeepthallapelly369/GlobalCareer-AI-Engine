# Corporate Earnings Guidance: Nifty 50 Bellwethers

## Tech Sector (TCS, Infosys)
- **Status:** Headwinds.
- **Guidance:** Both IT majors have quietly downgraded their Q1 revenue guidance to flat or negative. Discretionary spending in the US and European markets has stalled due to recessionary fears.
- **Hiring:** Net negative headcount addition; aggressive cost-cutting measures are in place.

## Banking Sector (HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank)
- **Status:** Mixed.
- **Guidance:** Credit growth remains strong at 15% YoY, but deposit growth is lagging at 10%. As systemic liquidity tightens, banks are fighting for deposits, narrowing Net Interest Margins (NIMs). An impending RBI rate hike would exacerbate the cost of funding.
- **Asset Quality:** Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) are currently stable, but unsecured retail loans are beginning to show slight stress.

## Energy Sector (Reliance Industries)
- **Status:** Strong.
- **Guidance:** Benefitting from resilient Gross Refining Margins (GRMs) and robust consumer demand in the retail and telecom divisions (Jio). Seen as a defensive play by institutional investors.
